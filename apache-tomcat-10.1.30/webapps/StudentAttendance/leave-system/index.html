Key Features of the System

📝

Digital Leave Application

Students fill an online form with reason, dates and type of leave.

✅

HOD Approval Workflow

HOD reviews each application and approves or rejects with remarks.

📊

Auto Attendance

Approved leaves automatically mark the student as "On Leave" in attendance.

🚫

Protected from Absent

Teachers cannot mark a student absent if they have an HOD-approved leave.

🔔

Status Tracking

Students can see the status of their leave: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

🔐

Secure Login

Separate login portals for Students, Teachers, and HOD with role-based access.
